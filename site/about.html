Twenty years in the industry.

RSMotoCons exists because dealer software keeps being built by people who have never desked a deal, chased a funding stip, or walked a lot at 7am counting aged units. We have. For twenty years, on every side of the business.

The Journey

Four chapters, one industry

Each stage of the road taught something the others couldn't — and all of it is built into the tools.

Independent Repairers

The start, and the hardest school. In an independent workshop nothing is given: every job has to be won, every part has to be costed right, and every customer comes back only if the work and the word are good. That's where respect for the customer relationship — and for every dollar — was learned.

Dealerships

Then the retail floor: inventory strategy, desking deals, building marketing that pulls real traffic, and structuring finance that funds. Years of month-end closes, Saturday floors, and the discipline of turning stock into gross — the operating knowledge at the heart of every RSMotoCons tool.

OEM

The manufacturer side showed the other half of the picture: how brands design programs, set standards, and measure their networks. Understanding what the factory actually wants from a dealer — and why — changes how you run one.

Factory Backed Operations

Finally, running factory-backed operations end to end — the place where OEM expectations meet retail reality every single day. Balancing brand standards, network targets, and a P&L teaches you exactly which processes matter and which are theatre.
