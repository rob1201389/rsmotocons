One platform.
Your whole dealership.

The RSMotoCons platform is a modern dealer management system in active development: each dealership runs as its own fully isolated operation, with inventory, leads, test drives, and online deposits working as one — and security engineered in from the first line of code.

The Modules

Built as one system, not bolted together

Four modules sharing one database, one customer record, and one source of truth.

Inventory Engine

Full vehicle records — VIN, rego, stock number, pricing, status — with automatic feed ingestion from third-party systems and instant reservation locking.

Lead Management

Every enquiry — web form, chatbot, classifieds, phone — captured, validated, assigned, and tracked on a full activity timeline through to sold or lost.

Test Drive Manager

Calendar scheduling with double-booking prevention, secure digital ID capture, and licence details encrypted the moment they're collected.

Online Deposits

Customers reserve a car with a holding deposit online. Funds route straight to the dealership's account, the vehicle locks instantly, and a tax invoice is generated automatically.

Security & Trust

Engineered like it's handling money — because it is

Isolated by design

Every dealership is a sealed tenant. Data isolation is enforced automatically on every single database query — one store can never see another's data.

Encrypted & access-controlled

Sensitive personal data is encrypted at rest with AES-256. Staff access is role-based — principal, manager, rep — with secure session handling.

Audited, always

Every staff access to customer and lead data is written to an immutable audit log — readiness for Australia's Notifiable Data Breaches scheme built in.

Payments done right

Card details never touch our servers — payments run through a PCI-DSS-compliant provider, with webhook signatures verified on every transaction.

Compliance posture: Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) & Australian Privacy Principles · Spam Act 2003 opt-in consent · PCI-DSS via Stripe · NDB-scheme audit readiness.
