Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

RSMotoCons ("we", "us", "our") provides dealership operations consulting and software tools from New South Wales, Australia. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we hold and protect it, who we disclose it to, and what you can ask us to do about it.

We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) in Schedule 1 of that Act, including the Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) scheme in Part IIIC.

1. Who this policy covers, and which law applies

RSMotoCons is a private sector business. The Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the APPs are the privacy law that applies to us.

Some businesses with an annual turnover of $3 million or less are "small business operators" and are exempt from the Privacy Act. We do not rely on that exemption: we apply the APPs to all personal information we handle, whether or not the exemption would be available to us.

The Privacy and Personal Information Protection Act 1998 (NSW) (PPIP Act) regulates NSW public sector agencies. It does not apply to us, and we do not claim compliance with it. Similarly, the Health Records and Information Privacy Act 2002 (NSW) (HRIP Act) applies to health information. We do not collect health information, so the HRIP Act does not apply to our handling of the information described in this policy. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before any health information is collected.

2. What personal information we collect

We collect only what we need for the purposes set out in section 3.

2.1 Website enquiries

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The department or area you are enquiring about
  • The content of your message
  • Whether you opted in to receive occasional updates
  • The date and time the enquiry was received, and the IP address it came from

2.2 Trade plate record of use

Where a client uses our trade plate record, drivers record each use of a trade plate. This is a record the plate holder is required to keep and produce on request. It contains:

  • The driver's name
  • The driver's licence number, if the driver chooses to enter it (optional)
  • The driver's handwritten signature, captured on screen as an image, on taking the plate and on returning it
  • The plate number, batch number, vehicle make and registration or VIN
  • The destination and purpose of the trip
  • The date and time the plate went out and came back, and any notes
  • The IP address and browser of the device used to make each entry, and the time the entry was saved

The device and timing information is collected so that an entry can later be shown to be genuine. It is part of what makes the record credible if it is ever produced to a regulator, insurer or court.

2.3 Information we do not collect

We do not collect health information, biometric information or biometric templates, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions or memberships, religious beliefs, philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, sexual orientation or practices, or criminal record. We do not collect payment card details through this website. We do not use the website to track you across other websites.

A handwritten signature is not biometric information for the purposes of the Privacy Act, but we treat it as sensitive in practice and protect it accordingly (see section 6).

2.4 Anonymity and pseudonymity (APP 2)

You can browse this website without identifying yourself. You can make a general enquiry using only an email address and a first name. You cannot use the trade plate record anonymously: identifying the driver is the purpose of the record, and it does not work without it. A driver who does not wish to use it can ask the office to make a paper entry instead; the same information is still collected, because the record must still be kept.

3. Why we collect it, and how we use it (APPs 3 and 6)

  • Enquiries: to respond to you, to understand what you need, and to keep a record of the conversation.
  • Trade plate records: to create and keep the record of use required for each trade plate, to produce it on lawful request, and so the plate holder knows which plate is out, who has it, and when it is due back.
  • Marketing: only if you opted in. Every marketing message includes an unsubscribe. We do not sell personal information, and we do not disclose it to third parties for their own marketing (APP 7).
  • Security and integrity: to detect and investigate misuse of our systems, and to keep an audit trail of who accessed records.

We do not use personal information for a secondary purpose unless you would reasonably expect it and it is related to the primary purpose, you have consented, or we are required or authorised by law.

We do not use personal information for automated decision-making that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

4. Unsolicited personal information (APP 4)

If we receive personal information we did not ask for and could not have collected lawfully for one of the purposes above, we destroy or de-identify it as soon as practicable, unless the law requires us to keep it.

5. Notification at the point of collection (APP 5)

Where we collect personal information directly, we tell you at that point who is collecting it, why, who it may be disclosed to, and how to reach us. The trade plate form shows a short collection notice beside the signature box, and links to this policy.

Where a client's staff enter a driver's details, that client is responsible for telling the driver. Our notice is displayed on the form to support them in doing so.

6. How we hold and protect it (APP 11)

These are the specific controls in place, not general assurances:

  • In transit: HTTPS only. HTTP Strict Transport Security is set with a one-year max-age, including subdomains, with preload.
  • At rest, infrastructure level: our database and key-value storage are encrypted at rest by our hosting provider (Cloudflare), as stated in their documentation. We rely on that statement; it is not something we can independently verify.
  • At rest, application level: in addition to the above, the sensitive content of each record is encrypted by our own application before it is written to the database, using AES-256-GCM. For trade plate records this covers the driver's name and licence number, both signatures, the vehicle, destination, purpose, notes, and the device information. For website enquiries it covers your name, email address and message. Only the fields needed to index and sort records — a plate number and timestamps — are stored unencrypted. A copy of the database on its own does not disclose the protected content.
  • Keys: encryption keys are held as secrets in our hosting platform's secret store, injected into the application at runtime. They are not in our source code, not in our source repository, and are never sent to your browser. We cannot read them back out of the platform.
  • Administrative access: the administrative area is protected at the network edge by Cloudflare Access, which authenticates the person before any request reaches the application. Administrative pages are additionally protected inside the application.
  • Sessions: session cookies are HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite=Lax, are cryptographically signed with HMAC-SHA256, and expire. Administrative sessions expire after two hours.
  • Rate limiting: repeated failed sign-in and PIN attempts from the same address are throttled.
  • Audit logging: we keep an append-only log of administrative sign-ins, record views, exports and changes, retained for two years. The log records who, what and when. It never contains record content.
  • Web application security: all database access uses parameterised queries; all output is escaped; a Content Security Policy restricts scripts to our own origin; clickjacking is blocked with frame-ancestors none and X-Frame-Options DENY; cross-site request forgery is mitigated by SameSite cookies, and by a same-origin check on state-changing requests in the signed-in areas; MIME sniffing is disabled; pages holding personal information are marked no-store and noindex.
  • Staff access: access to personal information is limited to the people who need it for the purposes above.

No system is perfectly secure. These controls reduce risk; they do not eliminate it.

7. Who we disclose it to (APP 6)

We do not sell personal information. We disclose it only:

  • to our service providers, who process it on our behalf under contract and only on our instructions (see section 8);
  • for trade plate records, to Transport for NSW, the NSW Police Force, an insurer handling a claim, or another body with a lawful entitlement to the record;
  • to our professional advisers where necessary;
  • where required or authorised by law, or to lessen or prevent a serious threat to life, health or safety;
  • to a buyer, in connection with a sale of our business, subject to equivalent privacy obligations.

Where we provide software to a client, the client is the entity responsible for the personal information in their own records. We act as their service provider for that data and handle it only on their instructions.

8. Service providers and overseas disclosure (APP 8)

We use the following providers:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — website delivery, application hosting, database and key storage, and access control. Cloudflare operates a global network; although we configure Australian storage where the service offers that choice, data may be processed on Cloudflare infrastructure in other countries, including the United States, as part of delivering the service.
  • GitHub, Inc. (a Microsoft company) — source code storage. Our source code contains no personal information and no secrets.

Before disclosing personal information to an overseas recipient we take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled consistently with the APPs, as APP 8.1 requires. We rely on contractual terms with these providers. Please note that an overseas recipient may be subject to the laws of its own jurisdiction, which may compel disclosure in ways Australian law would not.

If you would like the current list of providers and the countries involved at any time, ask us using the contact details in section 13.

9. Data quality (APP 10)

We take reasonable steps to ensure the personal information we hold is accurate, up to date, complete and relevant. Trade plate records are an historical record of an event and are not altered after the event; if a record is wrong, we attach a correction rather than rewriting history, so the audit value of the record is preserved.

10. How long we keep it, and how we destroy it (APP 11.2)

  • Website enquiries: retained for 12 months from receipt, then deleted automatically.
  • Trade plate records: retained for 5 years from the date of the trip, then deleted automatically. Records for plates that have not yet been returned are never deleted, because the record is not complete.
  • Audit logs: retained for 2 years.

Deletion is a hard deletion of the stored record, including the encrypted content; there is no hidden archive copy retained by our application. Backups are covered in section 11.

We may keep information longer where we are required to by law, or where it is needed for an actual or reasonably anticipated legal claim.

11. Backups

Our database provider retains point-in-time recovery data for a rolling 30-day window, held within the provider's encrypted storage. Because it is a rolling window, a record deleted today may persist in recovery data for up to 30 days before ageing out.

We do not currently export and keep our own separate backups. If we start doing so, the export will be encrypted before it leaves the platform and this policy will be updated to say so. Note that the content of each record is in any case encrypted by our application (section 6), so it remains encrypted inside any backup.

12. Data breaches (Part IIIC — Notifiable Data Breaches)

If we suspect a data breach we contain it, then assess it within 30 days. If we conclude there has been unauthorised access to, unauthorised disclosure of, or loss of personal information that is likely to result in serious harm, and we cannot prevent that harm through remedial action, we will notify the affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as soon as practicable, as Part IIIC requires.

Where we hold data on behalf of a client, we will notify that client promptly so they can meet their own obligations.

13. Access, correction and complaints (APPs 12 and 13)

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it. We will respond within a reasonable period, normally within 30 days. We do not charge for making a request. If we refuse access or correction, we will tell you why in writing and how to complain.

To make a request or a complaint, contact us at info@rsmotocons.com. We will acknowledge a complaint within 5 business days and aim to resolve it within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner: oaic.gov.au, or 1300 363 992.

Where the information is held in a client's trade plate record, ask us and we will direct your request to the client responsible for that record, or handle it on their instructions.

14. Cookies

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not run third-party analytics on this website. Where you sign in to a restricted area, we set strictly necessary session cookies so the site knows you are signed in. Blocking those cookies will prevent sign-in from working. Our access control provider sets its own session cookie for the same purpose.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed. Material changes affecting how we handle information already collected will be notified to affected individuals where practicable.
